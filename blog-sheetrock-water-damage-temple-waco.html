How to Repair Ceiling Sheetrock Damage From Water Leaks in Central Texas

Published for Homeowners in Temple, Waco, Belton, and Hewitt, TX

1. Identify and Stop the Leak Source

Before repairing any ceiling sheetrock in your Temple or Waco home, ensure the primary plumbing leak or roof leak is completely fixed. Damp drywall will yellow, sag, and promote mold growth if sealed prematurely.

2. Cut Out Damaged Drywall & Treat Subframe

Use a drywall saw to cut away sagging or discolored sheetrock in a clean square shape. Inspect ceiling joists for moisture and apply anti-microbial treatment if humidity was high.

3. Seamless Texture Matching (Knockdown & Orange Peel)

The biggest challenge in Central Texas drywall repair is matching existing acoustic textures. Professional contractors in Bell and McLennan counties use specialized spray equipment to blend new patches invisibly into surrounding ceilings.
