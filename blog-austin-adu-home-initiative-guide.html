The 2026 Homeowner's Guide to Austin's HOME Initiative & Garage Conversions

Published for Property Owners in Austin, Round Rock, Waco, and Central Texas

1. What is the Austin HOME Initiative?

Passed by the Austin City Council, the HOME (Home Options for Middle-income Development) Initiative allows single-family property owners to construct up to **two Accessory Dwelling Units (ADUs)** on a single lot, eliminating previous restrictive parking and lot-size caps.

2. Why Garage Conversions Offer Highest ROI

Converting an existing attached or detached 2-car garage costs **$60,000 to $125,000**—roughly half the cost of a ground-up build ($200,000+). Average monthly rental income for a 500 sq ft unit in Austin ranges from **$1,400 to $2,200/month**.

3. Permitting & Feasibility

With express review pathways for units under 1,100 sq ft, permit timelines have dropped from 9 months down to 45–90 days. Contractors handle utility hookups, electrical panels, and city inspections.
